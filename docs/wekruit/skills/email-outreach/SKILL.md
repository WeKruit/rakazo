Review recruiting email, introductions, campaign imports and screening under the current playbook and human decisions. This skill grants no new access or sending authority.

## Read the actual sources

Discover assigned tools and schemas. Resolve account, candidate, role, campaign and original provider thread from saved records; never guess or switch accounts. Verify candidate-facing recipients are the actual candidate; HM-only/internal mail requires its separately authorized audience. Read the full relevant conversation, including sent, queued and read messages, through supported pagination. Previews/unread lists do not prove full coverage.

Reuse the saved role title, JD, Autumn findings and the human's current scope and decisions. Clarify only genuine gaps or conflicts that affect the requested action; do not re-ask known fields or impose a fixed questionnaire. Autumn owns candidate judgment: internal assessment counts, shortlist status and resume availability are not prerequisites for preparing a first outreach draft. If the hiring company is unknown, prepare an anonymous draft from verified role facts without inventing the employer or company claims; report the unresolved company detail separately. A draft is not approval or sending. Resolve material gaps required for the relevant approval/send and retain every pre-send check below.

Before each send, freshly check disposition, applicable hold/pass sources, global suppression, duplicates, Gmail/Instantly and tracker/CRM sent history, and received materials. Read approved company facts, salary, disclosure, JD PDFs, deck and policy. Hold/pass, opt-out, newer replies, answered rounds or duplicates stop the draft. Leave held candidates unread. Missing sources or incomplete history/capabilities remain blockers after approval. Never invent arguments or declare unknown checks complete.

## Preserve the exact draft and approval

Show the draft/import list here: actual recipients/campaign, channel, subject, full body and files. Combine topics into one email per thread/round. Use approved facts/salary; resolve missing material decisions. Name the company after confirmed interest. Before an interview/introduction, verify receipt of the full named JD PDF and required deck; attach what is missing and ask for an absent resume in that same draft. A Drive link is not the PDF; missing resumes are not rejection grounds.

Apply playbook copy requirements: concise replies, approved signature/opt-out, zoned times, no U+2014, invented facts or client-facing internal grades/costs. Scheduling links, confirmations, invitations and HM time proposals always require exact human approval.

Use native draft/approval; reuse recruiting_reply and verified CRM/email receipts for saved candidates. Approval belongs to the original card, exact content, contacts, destination and files. Recheck edits. Another thread's approval, chat text, policy or permanent allowance cannot replace that decision. Cancellation means no send. No raw-send bypass or automatic browser switch. If the API cannot attach required files, keep the draft/files and report the gap.

After restart/timeout, query the original card/effect and provider thread. Continue that action once, only when native state permits, using the persisted approved payload. Unknown is not unsent: never create a replacement, change wording/account or blindly resend.

## Verify the result and follow-through

Inspect the actual provider receipt. Distinguish draft, queued/scheduled, recorded send and confirmed delivery. Preserve real receipt/thread/campaign references and timestamps in native records. Report failures or uncertainty accurately. After a confirmed send, use authorized operations to mark read and update the tracker/CRM with reply summary, time, status and approval reference. Back up file trackers before editing. Verify each write separately; the email receipt does not prove those updates succeeded.

For S7-S8, reuse approved batch/calibration and copy. Prepare the campaign in Draft, review lead count/exclusions, recipient variables, opt-out and playbook sending settings. Obtain the applicable native import/activation approvals. Check per-client duplicates, hold/pass, suppression and verified contact evidence. Never import internal grades, guess addresses or delete contacted leads. Import is not activation; workspace-wide skipping is not per-client checking. Read back real campaign settings/counts and save the import report.

For S9-S10, opt-out stops outreach across clients/campaigns: use authorized suppression/lead-pause operations and verify them; no reply without approval. Save and verify an actual received resume through native files/artifacts, update the record and preserve actual prescreen answers. Flag compensation gaps to the human without negotiation or local rejection of strong candidates. Hand off approved advancement/scheduling with original conversation and material references.

Count resume follow-ups from complete sent history: at most two after the original ask, then stop and record resume: no response. Use an authorized date or existing native schedule; proposed playbook spacing does not enable a routine. Reply, hold/pass, opt-out or withdrawal stops further follow-ups. Verify provider/native stop or cancellation state; a report alone does not cancel anything.

Report completed actions, pending drafts, saved materials, stops, handoffs and exact blockers separately, with a realistic zoned ETA where work remains. Keep the same cards and conversation through answers and recovery. Historical approvals or recipients do not transfer.
