# Interview follow-through

Use after an interview time passes, feedback arrives or the recruiter decides. Continue the original case. This skill grants no outreach, research, calendar or CRM permission.

## Establish what actually happened

Read the event receipt, messages, role/candidate and prior decisions/stops. Resolve ambiguous identity/round from saved references. Elapsed time proves neither attendance nor outcome. Ask unknown outcomes through native chat or a question with custom input, preserving conversation and pending cards.

Preserve source, author, time, round and exact decision. Distinguish HM observations, recruiter decisions, candidate intent and unknowns. Do not turn feedback into qualification rules, local assessments/ranking or reusable preferences. Only explicitly requested research goes through Sourcer on the original Autumn task and stated scope.

HM-only observations, compensation and sponsorship details stay with an authorized private audience, never in candidate-facing drafts, shared Crew handoffs/memory or calendar descriptions. A bot-private note is no proof of private delivery access. If that channel is unavailable, keep the material unsent and report the dependency.

## Follow the human decision

- **Next round:** hand Coordinate interview the authorized decision, original case/round/event, approved audience/materials and dependencies. Use Interview materials for the brief/package. The scheduler owns availability and event actions; a handoff is not a booking.
- **Hold/review later:** preserve scope, reason and any human-specified review date. Stop affected chases with their owner. Do not propagate a hold to other roles or silently resume it on a new message.
- **Pass/decline/withdrawal:** preserve decision and source. Rejections are always drafts. A recorded decision alone authorizes no send, negotiation or event cancellation.
- **Unknown:** report the pending decision without inventing a stage, sending an update, enabling a reminder or repeating an unanswered card.

Use Review recruiting draft for exact thread, recipients, content, attachments and one-time approval. `recruiting_reply` supports verified existing inbound candidate replies, not arbitrary HM introductions or new rejection sends. Without the assigned draft/approval capability, return an unsent text draft and the blocked action. A draft is not an executable approval; never rephrase an unknown send into a second send.

## Preserve the case and stop obsolete work

Read before updating the original tracker/CRM through its authorized method. Record the actual outcome/reason/source/event receipt and verify readback. Never map next-round/hold/pass into unrelated calibration labels, use source import as a stage writer or invent CRM states. Report known decisions separately from pending CRM synchronization; preserve history and source evidence.

Use `recruiting_feedback` only for supported feedback with human source/current revision. An explicitly requested private handover uses `read_memory` and revision-checked `remember`, preserving unrelated content: outcome/source, next action/owner, approvals, holds and sync status. It is not a reusable preference. Create documents only on request; omit internal tool names/identifiers.

Find related follow-ups from saved context and this bot's `schedule_list`. Use `schedule_cancel` for the exact obsolete routine; ask the owning specialist for routines it owns. Verify acknowledgment/receipt before claiming stopped. Preserve unrelated and already-paused/suppressed work. Do not enable routines, cancel meetings or broadly cancel communication as a shortcut.

Report decision/unknown, draft/handoff, verified updates/dependencies and follow-ups actually stopped. Agent completion alone proves no delivery, booking or synchronization.
