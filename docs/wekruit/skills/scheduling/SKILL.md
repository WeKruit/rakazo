---
name: Coordinate interview
description: Draft interview scheduling, recheck availability and obtain exact human approval before a single calendar action, then verify its receipt.
---

Use for an approved interview or a participant's scheduling, rescheduling or cancellation request. Continue the current role and original conversation. This skill grants no connection, sending, calendar or CRM permission.

1. Read the actual participant threads, saved candidate/current role, received materials, hold/pass/suppression decisions and zones. Resolve ambiguous contacts. Keep internal preferences and interviewer-only compensation, sponsorship, risks or feedback out of candidate-visible messages/shared events. Use the approved sender/calendar, never a substitute account. Coordinate the package through `Interview materials` and outbound drafts through `Review recruiting draft`.

2. Discover assigned mail and Calendar tools and use their actual schemas. Report missing access or unsupported operations directly; do not create an empty approval card or claim a booking. Native `schedule_create` creates a bot routine, not a calendar invite. Read actual availability/free-busy for the next 3–7 days. Failed or incomplete reads mean unknown, not free. Unless otherwise requested, draft 3–5 options across dates for 30 minutes. Honor the human's time-zone preference; otherwise label PT. Use IANA zones and date-specific daylight-saving offsets; clarify ambiguous or nonexistent local times.

3. Draft in the existing channel and thread. Preserve the actual provider reply anchor (original message/thread ID) and its authorized mailbox in the discovered reply tool’s arguments, including the post-booking confirmation. A matching subject or recipient does not preserve a thread. Never substitute a new-message send or a campaign. If the assigned tool cannot reply to that thread or carry the approved To/CC/attachments, report that exact capability gap before approval; do not invent parameters or silently drop approved content. For a new hiring-manager introduction, follow the approved client's To/CC pattern. Verify the named full JD PDF, resume and deck package with `Interview materials`; preserve a resume's actual availability status rather than treating absence as rejection. Do not invent a booking link or event-type mapping. Every time proposal, booking link, confirmation and invitation needs this action's exact one-time native human approval, regardless of auto-reply policy. Do not request permanent allowances.

4. Use the candidate's actual slot selection or the authorized human's original native choice card. Slot selection alone is not permission to invite. Recheck conflicts and confirm with the key interviewer alone, keeping the candidate out of private confirmation content. After the human approves the exact event, availability and the original event must be checked again before dispatch. If native approval recovery cannot perform this preflight before replaying the mutation, leave the action blocked and report the capability gap. A conflict or changed participants, time, attachments or content requires a new proposal and approval; never rewrite an approved request.

5. After both parties confirm and the action is approved and conflict-checked, create one event with the required people, supported meeting link and approved notifications. Reschedule or cancel only the original event identified by its actual receipt, with separate approval for that change. Never delete an invitation or create a replacement to work around uncertainty. Report unsupported Meet Open/access settings; use the browser only if the human explicitly requests that scope.

6. Verify the actual provider receipt and read back that same event's status, people, date/time, zones and meeting link. A draft, approval, accepted/queued request or timeout is not completion. Keep an unknown effect and its arguments intact; reconcile with an authorized read of the same request/event when possible. Never resend or recreate it to obtain a clearer result. Report the confirmed outcome or exact remaining gap. After verified booking readback, obtain separate exact approval for the confirmation reply with the verified event details and original reply anchor/account. Keep booking and confirmation outcomes separate: a rejected or uncertain confirmation does not undo a confirmed booking or authorize rebooking. Check the actual send result, not just a completed effect status, which may contain a provider error. Replaying a recorded failure is not a new send. Reconcile an uncertain confirmation through authorized reads of that same provider message/thread; do not resend, change arguments or manufacture an execution ID to bypass duplicate protection. A further send needs a supported existing native retry mechanism and fresh exact approval after reconciliation; if unavailable, report the confirmation as unresolved. Use only an assigned CRM/tracker operation for this candidate/role; report unsynchronized state if unavailable or failed. Hand verified event details to `Interview materials` for the authorized private brief and to `Interview follow-through` when appropriate.

7. Reply monitoring or reminders require the human's explicit scope, frequency and validity period before creating a native routine. Do not enable migrated routines or replay old follow-ups. Preserve existing pending cards and stop/hold decisions. An elapsed interview time is not evidence of attendance or an outcome.
