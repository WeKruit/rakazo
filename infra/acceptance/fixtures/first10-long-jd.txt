新岗位（测试）：Distribution Center Operations Manager — Atlanta, GA（现场办公）

About the company
Example Freight Co. is a fictional third-party logistics provider used for product testing. We run six distribution centers in the Southeast and handle e-commerce fulfillment, retail replenishment and cross-dock freight for consumer brands.

Role summary
The Distribution Center Operations Manager owns day-to-day performance of our 400,000 sq ft Atlanta-area facility. You lead three shift supervisors and about 120 hourly associates across receiving, putaway, picking, packing and outbound. You are accountable for safety, service levels, productivity and cost per unit, and you partner with the account team on peak planning.

What you will do
- Run daily operations across two shifts; plan labor against forecast volume and adjust in real time.
- Own KPIs: on-time shipping, order accuracy, units per hour, dock-to-stock time, inventory accuracy and safety incidents.
- Lead, coach and hire supervisors and leads; build a culture of accountability and recognition.
- Use the WMS (Manhattan, Blue Yonder or similar) and labor management reports to find and fix bottlenecks.
- Drive continuous improvement projects (slotting, pick path, 5S, standard work) with measurable savings.
- Prepare peak season plans, temporary labor ramp-up and staffing agency coordination.
- Keep the site audit-ready for OSHA and customer compliance reviews.

Requirements
- 5+ years of warehouse or distribution center operations management.
- Has managed teams of 50+ hourly associates through supervisors.
- Hands-on experience with a WMS and KPI-driven management.
- Experience in e-commerce fulfillment or 3PL environments.
- Must live within commuting distance of metro Atlanta; the role is fully on site.

Nice to have
- Mandarin Chinese (helpful for some customer accounts).
- Lean Six Sigma Green Belt or similar.
- Experience opening a new site or migrating to a new WMS.

Not a fit
- Agency recruiters or staffing consultants.
- Students or people without people-management experience.

Compensation: competitive base plus annual bonus; full benefits.
